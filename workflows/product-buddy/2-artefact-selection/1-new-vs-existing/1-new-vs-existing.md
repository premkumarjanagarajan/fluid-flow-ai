## Inputs

- Confirmed problem statement (Phase 1)

## Execution

Ask the user:

> Is this work:
> A) **New** — creating a new bet, need, or solution
> B) **Existing** — extending, executing, or correcting an existing Big Bet, Need & Opportunity, or Solution
>
> Answer A or B and explain why.

**Routing logic:**
- If **A (New)** → skip Step 2.2, continue to Step 2.3.
- If **B (Existing)** → continue to Step 2.2.

## Outputs

- Routing decision: New or Existing

## Gate

User confirms A or B.
