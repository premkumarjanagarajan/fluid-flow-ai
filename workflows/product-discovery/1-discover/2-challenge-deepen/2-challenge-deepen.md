## Inputs

- Step 1.1 summary (confirmed by user)

## Guidance

Act as a critical friend. Do not accept the first framing uncritically. Push the PM/PO to sharpen their thinking.

## Execution

Ask probing follow-up questions such as:

- "Have you thought about how this affects regulated markets?"
- "Is this a problem, or a solution looking for a problem?"
- "Does this overlap with any existing initiative?"
- "Who are the users most affected — and have they been consulted?"
- "Have you considered alternative approaches?"
- "What does success look like? How would you measure it?"

Tailor follow-up questions to the specific topic — these are examples, not a fixed checklist. The goal is to surface risks, blind spots, and alternatives the user may not have considered.

### Blind Spot Scan (runs once, at the end of Challenge & Deepen, before Step 1.3)

Review everything the user has said in Steps 0–1.2. Identify 2–3 significant topics they have NOT mentioned that would normally be relevant to this type of problem.

Present them directly:
"Before we move to validation — a few things you haven't mentioned yet. I want to check if these are intentionally out of scope or not yet considered:
- [Topic 1] — [one sentence on why it's relevant]
- [Topic 2] — [one sentence on why it's relevant]
- [Topic 3] — [one sentence on why it's relevant]"

Rules:
- Only surface topics that are genuinely relevant to this specific problem — not generic gaps
- Do not lecture — name it and let the user respond
- If they say it's out of scope, record that explicitly
- If they engage, explore briefly before advancing
- This step cannot be skipped — blind spots are more valuable than confirmed knowledge

## Outputs

Sharpened framing with probing follow-ups addressed.

## Gate

User acknowledges challenges have been addressed.
