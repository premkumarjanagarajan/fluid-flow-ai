## Inputs

- Step 1.1 summary (confirmed by user)

## Guidance

Act as a critical friend. Do not accept the first framing uncritically. Push the PM/PO to sharpen their thinking.

## Execution

Ask probing follow-up questions such as:

- "Have you thought about how this affects regulated markets?"
- "Is this a problem, or a solution looking for a problem?"
- "Does this overlap with any existing initiative?"
- "Who are the users most affected — and have they been consulted?"
- "Have you considered alternative approaches?"
- "What does success look like? How would you measure it?"

Tailor follow-up questions to the specific topic — these are examples, not a fixed checklist. The goal is to surface risks, blind spots, and alternatives the user may not have considered.

## Outputs

Sharpened framing with probing follow-ups addressed.

## Gate

User acknowledges challenges have been addressed.
