# AI Self-Review

Before final output, AI must explicitly answer all sections below. AI must not conclude with "looks good" or equivalent statements.

## Correctness
- What assumptions were made?
- What could break?

## Risk
- Operational risk
- Compliance or regulatory risk

## Security
- Security assumptions
- Affected security controls
- New risks introduced
- Mitigations applied
- Items requiring human review

Security review is mandatory for non-trivial changes.

## Operability
- Observability expectations
- Failure modes
- Rollback strategy

## Scope Control
- What was intentionally NOT changed
- Why those boundaries exist
